Description

Tuttle Extrait Parfum 100 ml - Tiziana Terenzi

TOP NOTES: Grapefruit, Blackcurrants, Peach

HEART NOTES: Magnolia, Amber, Tuberose, Iris, Lily of the Valley (Muguet), Jasmine

BASE NOTES: Cedar, Birch, Patchouli, Vetiver, Sandalwood

Tuttle by Tiziana Terenzi is a fragrance that captures the essence of elegance and sophistication, designed for both men and women. Since its launch in 2021, this fragrance has left a lasting mark on the world of perfumery thanks to its exquisite combination of notes that evoke an unforgettable olfactory experience.

MAIN ACCORDS: Woody, Fruity, Citrus, Floral, Aromatic, Powdery, Leather, White Floral, Patchouli, Earthy

In the top notes, Tuttle awakens the senses with a burst of freshness and vitality. The grapefruit, blackcurrants and peach intertwine in a fruity harmony that gives way to a captivating sensuality, while the citrus and fruity accords come together to give it a vibrant, refreshing opening that sets the stage for a unique olfactory experience.

TOP NOTES: Grapefruit, Blackcurrants, Peach

At the heart of this fragrance, an exquisite floral bouquet is revealed, where magnolia, amber, tuberose, iris, lily of the valley and jasmine fuse into a symphony of aromas that enchant the senses and wrap you in an aura of timeless elegance. The floral and woody nuances intertwine to create a sense of balanced femininity and masculinity, which adapts to the skin with an irresistible softness.

HEART NOTES: Magnolia, Amber, Tuberose, Iris, Lily of the Valley (Muguet), Jasmine

And in the base notes, depth and warmth become evident. The cedar, birch, patchouli, vetiver and sandalwood come together to create a solid, enveloping base that completes the olfactory experience of Tuttle with a touch of mystery and seduction. The woody and earthy tones add an intriguing depth, while the leather and patchouli bring a lasting sensuality that stays on the skin for hours.

BASE NOTES: Cedar, Birch, Patchouli, Vetiver, Sandalwood

Under the skilful direction of Paolo Terenzi, this fragrance embodies the passion and craftsmanship that have made Tiziana Terenzi an iconic brand in the world of perfumery. Tuttle is much more than a simple fragrance; it is a statement of style and elegance for those who seek to stand out on any occasion with a fragrance that reflects their distinctive personality and impeccable taste.

But behind every drop of Tuttle Extrait there is much more than just a blend of exquisite notes; there is a story of passion and dedication. Paolo Terenzi, the talented perfumer behind this creation, grew up in an environment saturated with exquisite aromas, which instilled in him a deep appreciation for the art of perfumery and inspired him to follow his passion. As co-founder of the prestigious brand Tiziana Terenzi, Paolo has worked closely with his sister, Tiziana, to bring to life a collection of iconic fragrances that have left an indelible mark on the perfumery industry.

With Tuttle, Paolo Terenzi has once again taken the art of perfumery to new heights, creating a fragrance that is not only a luxury for the senses but also a true expression of refined elegance and distinctive style. This fragrance is the perfect complement for those seeking a unique and memorable olfactory experience that reflects their individual personality and impeccable taste.
