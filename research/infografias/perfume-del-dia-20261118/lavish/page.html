Details on Boadicea the Victorious Lavish

Throw routine cares out of the window and let hope flourish with this extravagantly uplifting perfume, blessed with a bright buoyancy that elevates it high above the ordinary. Bergamot dashed with lemon gives a sense of expectancy; orange flower and rose are peppered with nutmeg to add a sumptuous, spicy centre; vetiver, guaiac wood and moss feature among a plethora of base notes designed to bring richness and refinement.

How does Boadicea the Victorious Lavish smell?

A sophisticated fresh-spicy composition that opens with vibrant citrus notes of bergamot and lemon. The heart reveals a harmonious blend of orange blossom, rose, and nutmeg, creating an elegant spicy-floral accord. The base is dominated by a rich vetiver note, complemented by cedar, moss, amber, and precious woods, resulting in a refined green-woody foundation.
