**Bois Mystique** by **Houbigant** is a Woody fragrance for men. **Bois Mystique** was launched in 2018. The nose behind this fragrance is Luca Maffei. Top notes are Cardamom, Pink Pepper, Ginger, Artemisia, Bergamot and Neroli; middle notes are Incense, Cypriol Oil or Nagarmotha, Cinnamon, Black Pepper and Iris; base notes are Cedar, Myrrh, Amber, Guaiac Wood, Cashmere Wood and Musk.

"With over four centuries of affluent history, Houbigant Paris still strives for excellence. We pride ourselves in creating innovative high quality products that will become the olfactory leader within that category and an iconic classic over time.

Bois Mystique is no exception. It presents itself as a rich woody fragrance with a distinct modern twist. The blended scent combines the freshness of the woods with a strong note of spices. It depicts a warm and comforting redolence that conjures a feeling of a toasty bonfire while appreciating the blazing pink and orange sunsets. With hints of liquors and exotic woodiness, leaving an intoxicating and irresistible sillage.

The top notes reveal a fresh accord of citrus notes with compliments of spices with bergamot, davana and ginger.  
 The heart notes consists of papyrus plant and the incense intertwined to reveal a mysterious spicy facet of the fragrance. The base notes with amber and woody undertones adds elegance to the fragrance character." - a note from the brand.

Top notes: bergamot, davana, neroli, cardamom, pink pepper, ginger fresh  
 Heart notes: black pepper, cinnamon, incense, cipryol, bois iris  
 Base notes: cashmerewood, guaiacwood, cedarwood, myrrh, amber, musk

The fragrance Bois Mystique is available as a 100ml Eau de Parfum and 100ml Extrait de Parfum.
