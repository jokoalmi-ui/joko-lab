Town & Country

CROWN COLLECTION

Clary Sage, Grey Amber & Sandalwood

25.00% Perfume Concentration

Originally created in 1925 and famously worn by Winston Churchill, Town & Country was a shining star of a new era of the Crown Perfumery Company. Today it is reimagined with the Clive Christian hallmarks of complexity, concentration and dedication to the finest ingredients - perfectly crafted for self expression. Crisp with refreshing notes from the most elegant of herbs resting on a deeper base. Capturing the natural beauty of the countryside and the elegance of London town.

perfume notes

Clary Sage

TOP NOTE
Clary Sage is a refreshing and invigorating top note, known for its herbaceous and slightly floral aroma. It adds a touch of freshness to the fragrance, evoking the scent of the English countryside.

Sandalwood

heart note
Sandalwood is a warm and woody heart note that adds depth and richness to the perfume. Sandalwood is a classic ingredient in perfumery and gives Town & Country a sense of sophistication and elegance.

Grey Amber

base note
Grey Amber is providing a subtle and sophisticated touch to the fragrance. It is a rare and precious ingredient that adds depth and complexity to the scent, evoking the warmth and comfort of a cosy fireside.
