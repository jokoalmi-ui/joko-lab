Description

Neroli Nostrum of Lunio

Departure notes: Ginger, Bergamot, Petitgrain

Half notes: Neroli, matured fig and tuberous

Notes background: Oud, Amber, Cedar of Virginia and White Sandalo de India

For centuries, Bitter Naranjo flowers have been valued by their unique fragrance, especially since the seventeenth century, when they began to distillen Neroli. This essential oil quickly became one of the favorites between the European elites, adorning the real courts with its distinctive and refined aroma.

Neroli Nostrum is an ode to this historical essence, capturing the elegance and sophistication that have characterized the neroli over the years. Currently, it remains one of the most coveted ingredients in the world of perfumery, appreciated by its freshness and its ability to evoke a timeless luxury aura.

To create Neroli Nostrum, a combination of the best raw materials has been carefully selected, each chosen to complement and enhance the essence of neroli. This meticulous selection of ingredients not only highlights the characteristic notes of neroli, but also adds a depth and complexity that make this perfume a true masterpiece.

The result is a perfume that encapsulates versatility and refinement, suitable for any occasion, but with a distinctive touch that distinguishes it from the rest. Neroli Nostrum is not just a perfume, it is an olfactory experience that reflects tradition and modernity in perfect harmony.

In addition, the Italian class touch that has been infused into the fragrance adds an element of sophistication that even further elevates its character. Italian influence is evident in every perfume nuance, from its bright opening until its persistent and enveloping bottom.

In short, Neroli Nostrum is not simply a recreation of traditional neroli, but a modern reinterpretation that maintains the essence of the past while looking to the future, offering a sensory experience that is both classical and contemporary.
